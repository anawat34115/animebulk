Eren Yeager's arc in Attack on Titan runs from an angry cadet who can barely stay upright in the ODM balance test to a hardened soldier. The Survey Corps' training (running in full gear, hand-to-hand combat, balance and endurance drills) is basic military fitness, and that's what this plan builds. It's an 8-week "cadet training" program combining strength, rucking and conditioning so you become tough, durable and capable rather than just big.

What Cadet Training Actually Builds

  • Work capacity: being able to keep going when tired, built with circuits and intervals.
  • Load-carrying strength: rucking (walking with a weighted backpack) and carries, the core of real military training.
  • Relative strength: pull-ups, push-ups and squats, the classic military fitness test movements.
  • Balance and core stability: Eren's ODM test is a balance test, and single-leg work and anti-rotation training are the real-world version.

The 8-Week Cadet Corps Plan

Four training days and one optional ruck. You need a pull-up bar, a pair of dumbbells and a sturdy backpack you can load with books or water bottles.

Two minutes of jogging in place, 10 leg swings each direction, 10 bodyweight squats, 10 push-ups, 5 scapular pull-ups and a 20-second single-leg balance on each side.

Day 5 (Optional): The Ruck

Walk with a loaded backpack. Start at 10% of your bodyweight for 30 minutes in week 1, and build to 15–20% for 60 minutes by week 8. Walk briskly, stand tall and keep the pack high and tight on your back.

Strength day: add reps to pull-ups and push-ups each week, then weight. Circuit: record your total time and beat it. Ruck: increase time first, then weight, never both in the same week. Week 8 is a test week: max pull-ups, max push-ups in 2 minutes and the 2.4 km run.
Rucking is low impact but builds up load on your feet and back. Wear good shoes, increase weight slowly and stop if you feel pain in your feet, knees or lower back.

Eren's Real Lesson: Keep Moving Forward

Eren fails the balance test at first because of broken equipment and keeps going anyway. The fitness version is simple: missed sessions and bad weeks don't matter; showing up next week does. Track your test numbers in week 1 and week 8. Most people improve their pull-ups by 3–5 reps and their 2.4 km time by a minute or more.

Eating for a Soldier's Body

Train at maintenance calories from the calorie calculator if you want to stay the same weight and get fitter, or add 200–300 if you're skinny and want to build muscle alongside your fitness. Protein at 0.7–1 g per pound of bodyweight (1.6–2.2 g per kg). Eat carbs before intervals and the circuit; they're your fuel.

Common Mistakes

  • Making every day a hard day. Intervals and the circuit are hard. The strength and balance days should feel controlled.
  • Rucking too heavy too soon. Time first, then weight.
  • Kipping pull-ups to hit numbers. Strict reps only; military tests don't count swinging reps.
  • Skipping the balance work. It's what keeps your ankles and knees healthy with all the running.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Survey Corps training realistic?

The basics are: running, rucking, balance work and hand-to-hand combat are all part of real military training. The ODM gear isn't, but balance and core training are the closest real equivalent.

Can I do this without running?

Swap intervals for bike or rowing intervals, or hill walking with the backpack. Keep the circuit and rucking.

How many pull-ups should I aim for?

A good goal for most men is 10 strict pull-ups; for most women, 3–5. If you can't do one yet, use bands and negatives and you'll get there.

Is rucking good for building muscle?

It builds endurance and strength in your legs, back and core rather than size. Combined with the strength days, it creates a tough, capable physique.
