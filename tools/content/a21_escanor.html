Escanor from The Seven Deadly Sins is the most extreme glow-up in anime: a skinny, nervous man at night, and by noon a giant of pure muscle who calls himself "The One." His daytime physique is all chest, shoulders and arms, the upper-body mass most people picture when they think "strong." You won't change size with the sun, but you can build a seriously impressive upper body. This 8-week program is an upper-body specialization plan with push, shoulder and arm focus, without skipping legs.

The Escanor Physique, Translated

Noon Escanor has a huge chest, round shoulders and thick arms. That's a classic bodybuilding upper body, and it's built with:

  • Heavy pressing for the chest and front delts: dumbbell bench press, incline press and dips.
  • High-volume shoulder work for width: lateral raises are the single most important exercise for round shoulders.
  • Direct arm work: curls and triceps extensions on top of the presses.
  • Enough back work to stay balanced, so the pressing doesn't round your shoulders forward.

The 8-Week "The One" Program

Four days a week: two upper-body push-focused days, one back-and-arms day and one lower-body day. You need adjustable dumbbells and a bench; a pull-up bar is strongly recommended.

Two minutes of light cardio, 15 band pull-aparts, 10 arm circles each way, 10 push-ups, then two light warm-up sets of your first press at 40% and 70% of your working weight.

Heavy day: add 2–5 lb (1–2 kg) per dumbbell whenever every set hits 8 reps. Volume day: add a rep per set each week until you reach the top of the range, then add weight. Week 5 is a lighter week: same weights, two sets per exercise. Weeks 6–8, aim to beat your week-4 numbers.
Shoulders take a beating in push-heavy programs. Keep your elbows about 45° from your body on presses (not flared out to 90°), never bounce dumbbells off your chest, and stop if you feel a pinch at the front of the shoulder. Pain-free reps only.

Why Lateral Raises Matter More Than Bench Press

The "big upper body" look comes mostly from shoulder width, and the side deltoids are what make shoulders wide. Presses train the front delts; only lateral movements hit the side delts directly. That's why this plan has 7–8 sets of lateral raises a week. Use a light weight, raise to shoulder height, lower slowly, and let the burn tell you it's working. Escanor's silhouette is a triangle; laterals draw it.

Eating for Noon-Escanor Size

Upper-body mass needs a calorie surplus. Use the bulking number from the calorie calculator (300–500 above maintenance) and keep protein at 0.7–1 g per pound of bodyweight (1.6–2.2 g per kg). Escanor drinks a lot of ale; you should not. Alcohol slows recovery and adds empty calories. Milk, rice, meat and the high-calorie smoothies are the better fuel.

Common Mistakes

  • Skipping leg day because Escanor's legs aren't the point. Training legs releases more growth-related hormones, burns more calories and keeps you from looking top-heavy.
  • Heavy, swinging lateral raises. Light weight, strict form, high reps.
  • Only pressing. Every push day needs balancing pull work, or your shoulders will roll forward and start hurting.
  • Ego-loading the bench. Pride is Escanor's sin. Leave one or two reps in the tank on most sets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I really build a big chest with dumbbells?

Yes. Dumbbell presses allow a deeper stretch than a barbell and train each side independently. With a bench and adjustable dumbbells up to 50–70 lb, you can build an impressive chest for years.

How long until I see a bigger upper body?

With consistent training and a calorie surplus, most people see visible changes in their shoulders and arms in 6–8 weeks, and a clearly bigger upper body after 6–12 months.

Do I need a bench?

It helps a lot for this plan. Without one, use floor presses, push-up variations and standing presses, but a basic adjustable bench is the best upgrade for upper-body training at home.

Is this plan good for beginners?

It's best after 2–3 months of general training. Beginners should start with the Deku workout or the free Shonen Bulk Starter, then move here.
